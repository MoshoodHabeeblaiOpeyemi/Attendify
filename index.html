📖 How Attendify Works

Your complete guide to managing or checking into lectures seamlessly.

🎓 For Students:

Join courses using the official code provided by your Department Rep. When a class is live, enter the 4-digit PIN and allow GPS location access to pass the 30m geofence check-in. Keep an eye on your 70% exam eligibility tracker!

👑 For Department Reps:

Register your School, Department, and Level to claim your department's admin slot. Generate 60-second rolling attendance PINs during lectures, manage enrolled students, appoint assistant reps, and export clean CSV attendance reports.

📍 GPS Geofencing & Security:

Attendify locks attendance to the lecture hall using GPS coordinates and short-window PINs, completely eliminating proxy sign-ins and fake check-ins.
